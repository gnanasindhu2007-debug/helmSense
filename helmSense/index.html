Venture code: VEN-HELM-6512

HelmSense is an innovative smart safety helmet designed to improve worker safety in industrial, construction, mining, and hazardous work environments. It combines traditional head protection with IoT-based sensors and intelligent monitoring technology. The system can monitor important safety conditions and help detect falls, impacts, abnormal environmental conditions, and worker emergencies.
